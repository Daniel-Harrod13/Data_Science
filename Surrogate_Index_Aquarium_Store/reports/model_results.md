# Surrogate Index Results

Aquarium store treatment: personalized aquarium care plan + coupon bundle.

Surrogate-index validation on historical customers:

- RMSE: $49.14
- MAE: $38.88
- R-squared: 0.899
- Correlation: 0.950

Estimated treatment effect before the 180-day outcome is available:

- Treatment mean surrogate index: $388.99
- Control mean surrogate index: $337.06
- Estimated ATE on surrogate index: $51.93
- True simulated 180-day ATE for validation: $56.91
- Treated customers: 2,005
- Control customers: 1,995

Interpretation: the treatment increases short-term behaviors that historically predict long-term value, so the surrogate index provides an early estimate of long-term impact.
